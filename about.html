Mixing the two

Study Part on Campus, Part Online

Cambridge Online is SWK Solutions taught live over the internet: the same teachers, not a separate staff or old recordings. Take Physics on campus at North Nazimabad on Tuesday and Mathematics online on Thursday. It suits students outside Karachi and anyone sitting a retake alongside school or work.

The method

How the Record Gets Made

The distinction record above is the outcome. This is the arrangement that produces it — the same four steps for every student, on campus or online.

Pick Your Subjects

Choose the teacher as well as the subject. Most students build a timetable across three or four of them.

Watch the Free Lecture

Every teacher's introduction chapter, free. Watch it before you decide — no form, no deposit.

Attend Live, Rewatch Later

Sessions run to the campus timetable and the recording is posted the same evening with the written notes.

Work the Archive

Past papers indexed by topic rather than by year, with mark schemes and marked model answers.

The institute behind it

Karachi Has Known This Name Since 2004

“Cambridge Online is not a separate brand. It is the SWK teaching experience, now open to students wherever they are.”

SWK Solutions has been teaching the Cambridge syllabus in Karachi since 2004, and it built its reputation the slow way: one distinction batch at a time, one teacher a student would travel across the city for.

The campuses now run across Karachi and Lahore — Gulshan‑e‑Iqbal, Gulistan‑e‑Johar, North Nazimabad and Bahadurabad in Karachi, and one in Lahore — with eighteen teachers across O Level, IGCSE and A Level.
